About CBAL LLC

CBAL LLC is a technology services company based in Cape Coral, Florida. We build and operate software solutions for community associations, property managers, and local organizations throughout Southwest Florida.

Our focus is on practical, privacy-first technology that helps communities operate more efficiently — from visitor management and parking systems to environmental monitoring and operational notifications.

What We Do

Community Operations & Notifications

We provide transactional SMS notification services for condominium and homeowner associations. These messages include visitor parking confirmations, community operational alerts, and time-sensitive notices that help residents and property managers stay informed.

Visitor Management Systems

Custom-built kiosk and check-in systems for gated communities and commercial properties, including visitor registration, badge printing, and automated notifications to hosts.

Environmental Monitoring

Water quality and environmental sensor networks for Southwest Florida communities and watershed organizations.

SMS Messaging Terms of Service

Effective: March 20, 2026

Program Description

CBAL LLC sends transactional and informational SMS messages on behalf of community associations we serve. Messages may include visitor parking pass confirmations, community operational notices, and time-sensitive alerts related to association operations.

Consent & Opt-In

You will only receive SMS messages from CBAL LLC if you have provided prior express consent. Consent is collected through written registration forms completed in person at the community association office. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of residency, membership, or any purchase.

By providing your mobile phone number and checking the opt-in box on the registration form, you agree to receive transactional text messages from CBAL LLC related to community operations. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a single confirmation message acknowledging your opt-out, and no further messages will be sent. Additional opt-out keywords accepted: STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT.

Help

For assistance, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at the information listed in the Contact section below.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on community activity. Typical recipients receive fewer than 10 messages per month. Messages are transactional only — we do not send marketing or promotional content.

Supported Carriers

Messages are sent via standard SMS and are compatible with all major US wireless carriers. Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.

No Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or provide your phone number or opt-in information to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers (such as our messaging platform) solely for the purpose of delivering messages on our behalf.

Effective: March 20, 2026

Data Security

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us.
